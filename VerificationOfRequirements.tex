\section{VERIFICATION OF REQUIREMENTS}

Possible verification methods include:
\bigskip

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Inspection:\\
	Visual examination, measurement, or gauging without special lab equipment.

	\item Demonstration:\\
	Showing that the system works by operating it.  No special instrumentation needed.

	\item Analysis:\\
	Review of documentation, calculations, or accumulated test data.

	\item Direct Test:\\
	Using special equipment or instrumentation to measure specific performance characteristics.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Interface Requirements}

\subsubsection{Physical}

\paragraph{}
Measure the probe with calipers.  Verify it fits within 5~cm $\times$ 5~cm $\times$ 5~cm.

\paragraph{}
Measure the boom length.  Verify it's between 30~cm and 60~cm.  Disconnect and reconnect the probe 10 times.  Verify the connection holds and the system still works.

\paragraph{}
Measure the handheld controller.  Verify it fits within 18~cm $\times$ 10~cm $\times$ 5~cm.  Weigh the full system on a scale.  Verify total is 1.5~kg or less.

\subsubsection{Functional}

\paragraph{}
Turn the steering control.  Verify the beamforming direction changes on the display.  Check the LCD datasheet.  Verify resolution is at least 320~$\times$~240.

\paragraph{}
Plug the system into a PC via the data port.  Verify the PC sees the device and data transfers.

\subsection{Functional Requirements}

\subsubsection{Sound Capture Module}

\paragraph{}
Inspect the mic array.  Verify three mics are arranged in a triangle.

\paragraph{}
Play test tone at 20~Hz with a calibrated speaker.  Record and verify the frequency shows up on the screen.

\paragraph{}
Play test tone at 20~kHz with a calibrated speaker.  Record and verify the frequency shows up on the screen.

\paragraph{}
Measure SNR on each channel using a 1~kHz tone at 94~dB SPL.  Verify all channels hit at least 60~dB.

\subsubsection{Signal Conditioning Module}

\paragraph{}
Feed a known signal into the preamp.  Verify the output level is in the ADC's input range.

\paragraph{}
Record a test signal and check the file properties.  Verify sample rate is at least 44.1~kHz at 16-bit or better.  Clap near the array.  Check the recorded arrival times on all three channels.  Verify the offsets match the known mic spacing, confirming the channels are in sync.

\subsubsection{Processing Module}

\paragraph{}
Place a sound source at a known position 2~m away.  Run the system and verify the estimated direction is within $\pm 5^{\circ}$ of actual.  Repeat at 5 different angles.

\paragraph{}
Put two sound sources at different directions.  Steer toward one and verify the other is at least 15~dB quieter in the output.

\paragraph{}
Watch the spectrogram for 10 seconds.  Count the frame updates.  Verify at least 10 frames per second.

\subsubsection{Storage Module}

\paragraph{}
Check the storage chip datasheet.  Verify at least 1~GB capacity.

\paragraph{}
Record audio and export.  Open the WAV and CSV files on a PC and verify they contain valid data.

\subsubsection{Display Module}

\paragraph{}
Run the system with a sound source at a known direction.  Verify the display shows a spectrogram with a directional indicator and a 3D arrow that matches the actual direction.  Move the source.  Verify the arrow updates to follow it.

\paragraph{}
Turn the steering control and time how long until the display updates.  Verify latency is under 100~ms.

\subsection{Non-Functional Requirements}

\subsubsection{Electrical}

\paragraph{}
Inspect the system.  Verify it runs on a rechargeable battery.  Charge fully and run continuously.  Time it until it dies.  Verify at least 2 hours.

\paragraph{}
Inspect for a USB-C port.  Plug in a charger and verify it charges.

\subsubsection{Physical}

\paragraph{}
Inspect the system.  Verify all electronics are enclosed.  Drop the controller and probe each from 1.2~m onto concrete.  Power on after each drop and verify everything still works.

\paragraph{}
Push on the boom sideways with a force gauge at 20~N.  Let go and verify it didn't bend.

\subsubsection{Environmental}

\paragraph{}
Run the system at 0$^{\circ}$C and 45$^{\circ}$C.  Verify it works at both extremes.

\paragraph{}
Run IP54 testing per IEC 60529 (dust and splash).  Verify the system still works after.

\subsubsection{Economic}

\paragraph{}
Add up the BOM.  Verify total component cost is \$500 or less.

\subsection{Other Requirements}

\subsubsection{Safety}

\paragraph{}
Inspect the system.  Verify no conductors are exposed during normal use.  Measure sound output at the user's ear with a sound level meter.  Verify it doesn't exceed 85~dBA.

\paragraph{}
Check the battery protection circuit datasheet.  Verify it has over-current and over-temperature protection.
